# Privacy Policy

Twine is an App created by Recurring Dream LLC for Apple platforms.
This policy applies to all information collected or submitted on Twine.

## Information Collection

Twine does not collect or retain any personal identifying information as part of its core functions. Twine does not create user accounts.

Twine stores your photos, reminders, and notes in the App's private storage on your device.

Twine uses your private iCloud storage to synchronize your data among your devices. This cannot be viewed by Recurring Dream LLC.

Twine uses Apple's in-app purchase mechanism, and may record the receipt of a sucessful purchase locally on the device.

Apple may collect crash logs if the privacy settings of your device allow it.

## Children’s Privacy

Twine is not designed to attract anyone under the age of 13. No identfying information is retained by Twine regardless of age.

By using Twine, you consent to this privacy policy.

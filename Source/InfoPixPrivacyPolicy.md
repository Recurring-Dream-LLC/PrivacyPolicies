## Privacy Policy

InfoPix is an App created by Recurring Dream LLC for Apple platforms.
This policy applies to all information collected or submitted on InfoPix.

## Information Collection

InfoPix does not collect or retain any personal identifying information as part of its core functions. InfoPix does not create user accounts.

InfoPix stores your photos, reminders, and notes in the App's private storage on your device.

InfoPix uses your private iCloud storage to synchronize your data among your devices. This cannot be viewed by Recurring Dream LLC.

InfoPix uses Apple's in-app purchase mechanism, which may record the receipt of a successful purchase locally on the device.

Apple may collect crash logs if the privacy settings of your device allow it.

## Children’s Privacy

InfoPix is not designed to attract anyone under the age of 13. No identifying information is retained by InfoPix regardless of age.

## Privacy Policy

Pacific is an app created by Recurring Dream LLC for Apple platforms.
This policy applies to all information collected or submitted on Pacific.

## Information Collection

Pacific uses Apple's Core Data and CloudKit technologies to store all user data.

Pacific stores your images, reactions, and follower information in the app’s storage on your device and in your iCloud storage on Apple's servers. 

When other users follow you, images and reactions are copied to their device storage and iCloud storage. That's how Apple's Core Data and CloudKit technologies accomplish sharing. 

Recurring Dream LLC cannot access your images or any other data. None of your data is stored by Recurring Dream LLC; we don't have any servers.

Pacific does not create user accounts. It uses anonymized iCloud identities to identify you and other Pacific users. Those identities are never retained by Recurring Dream LLC.

Apple may collect crash logs if the privacy settings of your device allow it.

## Children’s Privacy

Pacific is not designed to attract anyone under the age of 13. No identifying information is retained by Recurring Dream LLC regardless of age.

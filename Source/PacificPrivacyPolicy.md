## Privacy Policy

Pacific is an App created by Recurring Dream LLC for Apple platforms.
This policy applies to all information collected or submitted on Pacific.

## Information Collection

Pacific uses Apple's Core Data and CloudKit technologies to store all user data.

Pacific stores your images, reactions, and follower information in the App's storage on your device and in your iCloud storage. When other users follow you, that information is copied to their device storage and iCloud storage. That's how Apple's Core Data and CloudKit accomplish sharing. None of your data is stored by Recurring Dream LLC, we don't have any servers. 

Pacific does not create user accounts. It uses anonymized iCloud identities to identify you and other Pacific users.

Recurring Dream LLC cannot see your pictures or any other data. Apple may be able to access your pictures or data stored on their servers if required by law enforcement. Images and other data are not encrypted, and might be accessed by a person who has access to your devices.

Pacific uses Apple's in-app purchase mechanism, which may record the receipt of a successful purchase locally on the device.

Apple may collect crash logs if the privacy settings of your device allow it.

## Children’s Privacy

Pacific is not designed to attract anyone under the age of 13. No identifying information is retained by Recurring Dream LLC regardless of age.
